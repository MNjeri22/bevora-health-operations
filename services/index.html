01

Clinical Documentation Support

Assessments, treatment plans, progress notes, case management notes, quick notes, group/individual notes and discharge summaries all need attention. We help format documents, do completeness checks, and tracking and administrative follow-up. Clinical judgment and final approval remain with qualified clinical personnel.

02

Authorizations & Utilization Support

We help prepare submissions, track deadlines and follow up on request status. For ongoing services, we coordinate continued-stay paperwork and flag approaching expiration dates so your team knows what needs attention. We keep leaders informed about pending requests, conflicting services and next steps.

03

Billing & Revenue-Cycle Support

We help coordinate eligibility checks, review whether claims have the information needed for submission and support your billing workflow. Our support also includes tracking denials,payment reconciliation and preparing reports that show what is outstanding and where follow-up is needed.

04

Credentialing & Provider Enrollment

We help in CAQH profile maintenance, document collection, application preparation, payer follow-up, roster updates, recredentialing and expiration tracking. Approval timelines remain controlled by payers and regulators.

05

Intake, Scheduling & Administration

We support referral logging, initial outreach, intake-packet follow-up, appointment coordination, missing-information escalation, communication logs, inbox management and status reporting.

06

Compliance & Accreditation Readiness

We help review documentation, identify incomplete records, organize policies and track corrective actions. For CARF, Joint Commission, NHS and CQC accreditation bodies, we help organize supporting evidence and prepare for mock audits.

07

Training & Staff Development

We support onboarding, documentation training and orientation to your everyday workflows. We help develop practical checklists, standard operating procedures and refresher resources that staff can use in their work. We also track training completion.

08

Workflow Automation & Systems

We help set up systems for inquiries/lead capture, referral notifications, task assignments and deadline reminders. Depending on your needs, this can include CRM setup, dashboards, authorization alerts, trackers, escalation procedures and management reporting.
